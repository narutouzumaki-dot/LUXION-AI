    Privacy

    Your chats are stored in your private Supabase database, readable only by your account. Messages you send are also sent to Google Gemini to generate replies. Do not share passwords or secrets in chat.

    Attached files are read in your browser. Their text (or a resized copy of an image) is sent to Google Gemini only when you press Send. File contents are not saved to your chat history, only a note that a file was attached.

    Voice input uses your browser's speech service: in Chrome, your microphone audio is sent to Google to be turned into text. It only listens after you tap the mic, and you can stop it any time.

    Account deletion is not available in the app yet. To delete your account, ask the project owner to remove it in the Supabase dashboard (Authentication → Users).
